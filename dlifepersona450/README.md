### Hello, I’m DLife.

I’m a curious person behind an anonymous name. I explore technology, follow interesting ideas, and see what happens when different concepts meet.

Sometimes that becomes a website. Sometimes a game, an app, or a small experiment that leads somewhere unexpected. I’m also drawn to adventure: unfamiliar places, new perspectives, and the things you notice along the way.

### What I like to explore

- **Creative websites** — spaces to wander through, with interactions that tell a story.
- **Games and apps** — ideas you can play with, use, and build on.
- **Unexpected combinations** — bringing different concepts together to find a new direction.
- **Adventure** — collecting experiences and letting them shape what I make next.

### On the workbench

**[TIMEFOLD — The Folded Worlds](https://github.com/dlifepersona450/DLife/tree/main/timefold)**

A WebGL experiment combining a 2.5D diorama, a personal archive, and time you can navigate. Travel between eras to watch the room, its objects, and a traveller change with time.

The first version is built. There’s still room to explore, refine, and learn.
