FICTIONAL NOTEBOOK / 14 SEPTEMBER 1998 / AGE 10

A secret door
behind the desk.

I drew a map of the room today. The real room has one door. My map has three.

One goes to the garden. One goes to a mountain I saw from a car window. And one goes to a place where all the things I make can stay, even after I grow up.

Maybe a computer can be a room, too.
